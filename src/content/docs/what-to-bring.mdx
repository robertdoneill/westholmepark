Your room comes furnished with a **bed, closet, desk, and chair**. Here's what to bring to make it feel like home.

## Bedding & Sheets

We do **not** provide sheets. You can buy a set from us or order from Target or Amazon for delivery before you arrive.

- Sheets that fit your mattress — **Twin or Full** (confirm your room's size with us if you're unsure)
- Comforter & blankets
- Mattress pad
- Pillows & pillowcases

> **Buy sheets from us:** $69 (Twin) / $75 (Full).

## What to Bring

- 1–2 sets of sheets
- Comforter & blankets
- Mattress pad
- Pillows & pillowcases
- Shower shoes & caddy
- Towels (bath, hand, washcloth)
- Toiletries
- Laundry detergent & bag
- Small bins & hangers
- Laptop & chargers
- Surge protector
- Fan or desk lamp
- First aid kit
- Reusable bottle & utensils

## Leave These at Home

- Appliances with exposed heating elements
- Candles or incense
- Pets
- Wall-damaging adhesives (LED or 3M strips)
