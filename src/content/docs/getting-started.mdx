Welcome to Westholme Park! Here's what you need to know to get settled.

## Who We Serve

Westholme Park provides housing for female students attending UCLA, UCLA Extension, and Santa Monica College. Our community includes both graduate and undergraduate students, with private and shared bedroom options.

## Application Requirements

To reserve your spot, you'll need to provide:

- A valid passport
- A U.S. visa or school/work authorization
- Proof of funds

**Proof of funds** can be any of the following:
- Financial aid or student loans
- Pay stubs from U.S. employment
- College acceptance letter
- Bank statement showing sufficient funds for your stay

## How to Apply

1. **Submit your application** through our [online form](https://stay.havencoliving.com/apply) or email **join@westholmepark.com**.
2. **Schedule a tour** — virtual or in-person — to see the property and room options.
3. **Sign your lease** and submit your deposit to secure your room.
4. **Move in** on your scheduled date!

## Renter's Insurance

Per your Membership Agreement, you must maintain renter's insurance with minimum coverage of **$25,000** personal property and **$100,000** liability. Upload your declarations page to your Rentvine resident portal within **10 days** of move-in.

## Key Community Rules

- **Quiet hours** — 10 PM to 8 AM
- **Smoke-free & drug-free** property
- **Guests** — day visits are welcome; overnight stays require approval
- **Shared spaces** — clean up after yourself
- **Parking** — not included; please arrange offsite
- **Security** — never prop doors open or share access codes

## Move-In Day

Access codes are sent approximately **24 hours before** your move-in date. For what to pack, see the [What to Bring](/docs/what-to-bring/) checklist.
