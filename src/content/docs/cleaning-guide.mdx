This guide is provided so you can properly clean your spaces before vacating. Followed properly, it helps ensure you receive the maximum return of your deposit. Check off each item as you complete it.

Any item not completed by the member may be charged at the rates listed below.

## Bedroom & Closet

- [ ] Remove trash
- [ ] Strip and remove all bedding (keep it or dispose in the dumpster)
- [ ] Ensure the mattress is clean and free of all stains
- [ ] Empty and clean drawers, closets, and shelves — remove all debris
- [ ] Scrub all surfaces
- [ ] Check under the mattress for debris, items, and dirt
- [ ] Remove all personal items, clothing, and belongings
